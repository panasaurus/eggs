    From egg to server.

    An egg tells your panel how to install and run a game or application. Getting one in takes a moment.

    1. 1

      Find your egg.

      Search the collection and download its JSON file.

    2. 2

      Give it a nest.

      In your panel, open Admin → Nests and choose or create a nest.

    3. 3

      Import. Then make it yours.

      Choose Import, upload the JSON, and save. Your egg is ready for a new server.
